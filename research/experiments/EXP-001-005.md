# EXP-001 … EXP-005 — baseline ladder

- **Hypothesis:** each rung of the baseline ladder (single → mean → median → flow → confidence) changes
  noise, ghosting and detail in a measurable, explainable way.
- **Protocol:** `hie bench synthetic --split test` (ground truth) and `hie bench hdrplus` (12 real bursts, HDR+
  reference frames). The presets are in `hie_core/pipeline/config.py`. Parameters were tuned on the `tune` split only.
- **Results:** [docs/benchmark.md](../../docs/benchmark.md).
