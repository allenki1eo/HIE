What the metrics can and cannot tell you

Noise reduction

Immerkær noise σ in flat regions of the merged raw, relative to the reference frame, in dB. Warping with sub-pixel interpolation also smooths a little, so aligned merges can exceed the ideal 10·log10(N) gain.

Reference deviation

Share of pixels where the low-passed merge differs from the low-passed reference frame by more than 5σ of the reference's own noise. It catches ghosts, misalignment blur and lost detail. It is a proxy, not a ghost detector.

Agreement with Google merge

PSNR of the merged raw against Google's merged.dng after a least-squares gain fit. It measures similarity to one production system, not quality. Google's merge is not ground truth.

Detail retention

Share of the ground truth's fine band-pass detail present in the output. PSNR rewards smoothing, and this metric exists to catch that. HIE v0.1 trades a few points of detail for its PSNR gain.
