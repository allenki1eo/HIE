# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Hanson Image Engine to be a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a healthy project:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the research and the people doing it
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, commits, and
discussions) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers. Do not file a
public issue with private details. Contact
[@allenki1eo](https://github.com/allenki1eo) on GitHub, or open a private
security advisory on https://github.com/allenki1eo/HIE if the report itself
should stay off the public tracker.

Maintainers will review the report and may remove comments, commits, or
contributions, or ban a participant. Reports are kept as private as the
reporter asks.
